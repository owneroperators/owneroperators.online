OWNER/OPERATORS are happy to announce our newest song: _ECHOES AND STATIC_. We put this one together while we were trying to figure out how many times someone could be wrong about us before they just stopped asking altogether.

It feels exactly like being asked by a stranger what kind of band we are. And I'm going to tell them something different each time because why should anyone have an answer? Because that's what happens when you try too hard to fit inside a box someone else drew for you instead of drawing boxes around yourself where no one can see you without permission.

The tracklist for the album will feature ten songs named after various feelings about love which aren't really feelings but more like shapes made from feeling parts stacked up until the stack collapses into static.

We're uploading everything ourselves tonight so nobody else has control over the sound quality or who hears it first. It might get mixed differently on Spotify than it did here but at least here there was never any doubt about whether we intended to make music loud enough to hurt things listening back later on regret.

Thank everyone already reaching out with thoughtful questions and sorry if none of these answers match anything anybody expects - especially not what you expected us to expect you expecting.

-Gloria
