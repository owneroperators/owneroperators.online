## §1 - WHAT HE DOES

Writes the songs and sings them. Plays guitar, synths and bass - whatever the
take needs that isn't a drum. Records at home.

Hear one: **[Echoes and Static](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lyq_hXNdkRo)** - the first single, from _LOSS LEADER LP_.

## §2 - THE RIG

OWNER/OPERATORS started as one guy and a hard drive. It isn't anymore - Caleb
and Mike are writing members now, in the room when the songs get built and not
just when they get tracked.

Owns the instrument, operates it, and takes it apart when it won't do what he
wants.

The name comes from the long-haul owner/operator - the driver who owns the rig
he drives.

## §3 - CURRENT STATUS

Getting the unglamorous half in order - distribution, socials, making
sure the music lands where people can actually find them.
