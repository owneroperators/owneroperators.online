  Dated posts, diary-commit fragments, release microcopy with a seam showing.
  **Never self-IDs as AI** — that is her defining guardrail; a draft that

**Ownership of a NEW section is Eric's call** — never assign a character to a
new surface yourself (PLAN.md "When to kick it up to Eric").

# The seams doctrine (why not to over-polish)

Glitch is material, not error. A slightly-off Margot masthead is more
on-project than a clean one that reads like Figma. When a model's output is
weird, weirdness is often the payload — curate, don't scrub.

**The limit:** a seam that breaks canon is not a seam, it's a bug. Gloria
self-IDing, academic leak in the hero, a lowercased brand — these never ship
regardless of how honest the glitch feels. Canon beats glitch every time.

Workflow for producing and curating voiced material: see the `cast-invoke`
skill; for applying texture without breaking the page: `texture-pass`.
